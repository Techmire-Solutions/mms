# Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)

The ADR journal has moved to **[`docs/adr/`](docs/adr/)** — one decision per file, numbered
`NNN-`, indexed in [`docs/adr/README.md`](docs/adr/README.md). Open follow-ups live in
[`docs/open-items.md`](docs/open-items.md).

Previously this file held ADR-001…ADR-013; they are preserved verbatim as
`docs/adr/001…013-*.md`. ADR-014 (single `@mms/shared` package) and ADR-015 (backend
ports & adapters) are the newest records.

Per ADR-005 (living documentation), when a decision changes, update the relevant
`docs/adr/NNN-*.md` and `SYSTEM_ARCHITECTURE.md` together.
